# Student-Performance-Analysis-and-Bank-Customer-Mobile-App-Adoption-Analysis
# IDS Lab 1 – Data Analysis with Pandas

## Overview

This project demonstrates basic data analysis and visualization using **Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib**.

The notebook contains two real-world style case studies:

1. **Student Performance Analysis**
2. **Bank Customer Mobile App Adoption Analysis**

The goal is to practice data filtering, aggregation, percentage calculations, cross-tabulation, and visualization.

* Python
* Pandas

# Part 1: Student Performance Analysis

## Dataset Features

* Student_ID
* Attendance
* On_Time
* Participation
* Grade

## Analysis Performed

* Students who received Grade A
* Students with attendance above 80%
* Students with high participation
* Students who submitted assignments late
* Average attendance of Grade A students
* Average attendance of failed students
* Relationship between assignment submission and grades
* Percentage of Grade A students by participation level
* Percentage of Grade A students with high attendance

## Visualization

A bar chart is generated to show the percentage of students receiving Grade A under different conditions.

---

# Part 2: Bank Customer App Adoption Analysis

## Dataset Features

* Customer_ID
* Pays_Bills_Online
* Loan_Digital
* ATM_Only
* Adopts_App

## Analysis Performed

* Customers who adopted the mobile app
* Customers paying bills online
* Customers making digital loan payments
* ATM-only customers
* Relationship between online bill payments and app adoption
* Relationship between digital loan payments and app adoption
* Relationship between ATM-only behavior and app adoption
* Adoption percentages for different customer groups

## Visualization

A bar chart is generated showing mobile app adoption percentages among different customer categories.
